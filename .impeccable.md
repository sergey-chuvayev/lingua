## Design Context

### Users
A public atlas for people exploring the geographic distribution of languages. The user requested a clean, polished, responsive interactive map. The general-reader audience is a working assumption; no specific audience was supplied.

### Brand Personality
Curious, considered, clear. Make exploration inviting and estimates honest.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm editorial atlas: ivory paper, deep ink, muted teal choropleth, Instrument Serif headings and DM Sans controls. Large map, restrained borders, generous space. This direction was proposed with an optional preference question and is the implementation default.

### Design Principles
- Map first, with immediate language switching.
- Missing data is a visible state, never silently zero.
- Pair visual exploration with accessible searchable lists.
- Show provenance and coverage close to the numbers.
- Bundle every runtime asset for offline use.
